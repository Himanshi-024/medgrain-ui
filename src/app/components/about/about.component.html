About Medgrain

Premium quality flour for every Indian kitchen

Medgrain is a flour and food products brand of Sustainzy Agro Industries LLP, committed to delivering quality food products with a focus on purity, nutrition, hygiene and consistency.

Our products are manufactured with carefully selected raw materials and processed under hygienic conditions using modern food-processing technology. We aim to make quality flour accessible to households, retailers, restaurants, institutions and food businesses across India.

“From carefully selected grains to your kitchen — quality you can trust in every pack.”
